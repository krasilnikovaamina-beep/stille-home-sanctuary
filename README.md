# STILLE Home Sanctuary

Create a premium Danish home care and cleaning service website for a brand called STILLE.

The website should feel like a luxury interior, hospitality and lifestyle brand — not like a typical cleaning company.

Brand direction

Brand name: STILLE

Domain: stillehome.dk

Market: Aarhus and surrounding areas, Denmark.

STILLE provides discreet, high-quality home cleaning and home care for private clients.

The emotional concept of the brand is:
quiet luxury, calm, space, texture, scent, order, privacy and the feeling of coming home to a perfectly cared-for space.

We do not want to compete on low price.
We want the website to communicate quality, trust, discretion and exceptional attention to detail.

Avoid typical cleaning-industry visuals such as:
bright blue colours, cleaning-product graphics, cartoon icons, buckets, gloves, exaggerated before/after photos or generic smiling cleaners.

Visual style

Create a very minimal Scandinavian luxury aesthetic.

Colour palette:

warm white / ivory

black

soft stone grey

very subtle beige tones if needed

No bright colours.

Use lots of whitespace.

Typography should feel editorial and sophisticated:
an elegant serif font for large headlines combined with a clean modern sans-serif for smaller text.

Large typography.
Very little text on screen at once.
Strong photography.
Slow, calm visual rhythm.

The overall feeling should resemble a premium Scandinavian interior design brand, boutique hotel or luxury concierge service.

Important design instructions

Do NOT make it look like a SaaS website.

Do NOT use colourful cards.

Avoid excessive rounded corners.

Avoid gradients.

Avoid icon-heavy layouts.

Avoid stock-photo-looking cleaning images.

Use editorial full-width photography of elegant Scandinavian homes, natural materials, linen, stone, wood, bathrooms, kitchens and carefully styled details.

Animations should be very subtle: soft fades, gentle image movement and understated hover effects.

Mobile version must feel equally premium and spacious.

Build the complete homepage first.

Use Danish for all customer-facing website copy.
